MotoMastery Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

MotoMastery is a motorcycle riding course app published by Driss Bouksim (Mastery Software). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with Quebec's Law 25 and the GDPR.

1. Who is responsible

Driss Bouksim (Mastery Software), person in charge of the protection of personal information. Contact: support@motomastery.app

2. Information we collect

  • Your account: email address, display name and password (stored encrypted, we never have access to it). With Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address provided by that service. Country and province only if you enter them.
  • Your progress: completed lessons, quiz and exam results, streaks and study time, lessons marked "To review" and missed questions.
  • To run the app: a device identifier to send you reminders, and the usual technical information of any internet connection.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment data. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools.

3. Why

  • To create and secure your account
  • To save your progress and restore it on another device
  • To send you study reminders (can be turned off in Settings > Notifications)

We do not sell or rent your information.

4. Hosting

Your information is hosted by our technical providers, mainly Google, which may process it outside Quebec and Canada, notably in the United States. No one else has access to it, except where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Your information is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and progress are erased immediately.

6. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • Delete your account and your data: Settings > Delete my account
  • Ask to access, correct or get a copy of your information: support@motomastery.app
  • Turn off notifications: Settings > Notifications
  • File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (or your country's authority)

7. Security

Communication is encrypted and each user can only access their own data.

8. Minors

If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree before you create an account.

9. Changes

The update date is shown at the top. You will be informed in the app of any significant change.
