MotoMastery Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

MotoMastery is a motorcycle riding course app published by Driss Bouksim (Mastery Software). This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights. It is written in particular to meet Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) and the GDPR.

1. Who is responsible

  • Publisher: Driss Bouksim (Mastery Software)
  • Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Driss Bouksim, founder
  • Contact: support@motomastery.app

2. Information we collect

Account

  • Email address and display name;
  • password (email account): we never see it, it is stored encrypted by Firebase Authentication;
  • Google sign-in: name and email address provided by Google;
  • Sign in with Apple: name and email address provided by Apple; if you choose to hide your email, we only receive a relay address created by Apple;
  • country and province, only if you enter them (optional).

Progress in the app

  • Completed lessons, quiz and exam results;
  • activity streaks and study time;
  • lessons bookmarked "To review" and missed questions, so you can review them;
  • reminders already sent (so they are not repeated).

Technical data

  • A device identifier for notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging);
  • a proof that the app is genuine (Firebase App Check, through Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest), which protects the content against automated access;
  • your IP address, seen by the services that deliver the content (Google Firebase, GitHub for course images).

We do not collect location, contacts, photos or payment data. The app uses no analytics or advertising tools. Reading lessons aloud uses your device's speech engine: the text is not sent to us.

3. Why

  • To create and secure your account;
  • to save your progress and restore it on another device;
  • to send you study reminders (can be turned off in Settings > Notifications);
  • to protect the course content against automated copying.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where and with whom

Your information is hosted by Google Firebase (authentication, Firestore database, notifications, App Check). Course images are served by GitHub. These providers may process data outside Quebec and Canada, notably in the United States, under their own data protection commitments. No other third party has access to it, except where required by law.

5. Retention

Your information is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and progress are erased immediately from our database and from your device; the providers' technical copies are removed according to their own schedules.

6. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • delete your account and your data from the app: Settings > Delete my account;
  • ask to access, correct or get a copy of your information by writing to support@motomastery.app;
  • withdraw your consent to notifications in Settings > Notifications;
  • file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (or your country's data protection authority).

7. Security

Communication with our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Course content is encrypted on your device. Database access is restricted: each user can only read and change their own profile.

8. Minors

The app is intended for people preparing for a motorcycle licence. If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree before you create an account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The update date is shown at the top; you will be informed in the app of any significant change.
