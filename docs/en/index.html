Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MotoMastery an official app?

No. MotoMastery is an independent app, not affiliated with the SAAQ. It helps you prepare for Quebec's motorcycle knowledge test; for the official rules, see the SAAQ guide.

Is the app in English?

The interface is in English or French, but the lessons and exams are in French, like the test content you study.

What is free?

The 12 modules and their lessons, the 9 topic exams, the 204-sign directory and mock exams. Premium adds exclusive questions, unlimited mock exams and targeted review, as one purchase with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

Not to start: you can try the app without an account. A free account keeps your progress and brings it to all your devices.

What happens to my data?

No ads. You can delete your account and all your data at any time, right from the app.
