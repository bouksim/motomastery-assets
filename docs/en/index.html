The route

Three stages to the test.

Learn, check what you know, then practise in real exam conditions.

1

Stage 1

Learn

Everything the test covers, split into short illustrated lessons.

  • Complete lessonsGear, handling, lines, traffic, weather, mechanics, emergencies and regulations.
  • Read or listenEvery lesson can be read aloud, for another way to review.
2

Stage 2

Check

A quiz at the end of lessons and modules, with an explanation for every answer.

  • Explained quizzesYou know right away why an answer is the right one.
  • "To review"Your mistakes are set aside: review them until you master them.
3

Stage 3

Practise

Topic exams and a timed mock exam, with different questions each time.

  • Detailed correctionsEvery question reviewed at the end of the exam.
  • Stay motivatedProgress by module, study streaks and reminders you can turn off.
Finish line: the knowledge test.
