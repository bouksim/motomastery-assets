Delete your MotoMastery account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

From the app

  1. Open MotoMastery and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm your identity (password, or sign in again with Google or Apple), then tap Delete permanently.

Without the app

Write to support@motomastery.app from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data deleted

  • your account (email address, name, password);
  • your profile (country, province);
  • all your progress: lessons, quizzes, exams, streaks, "To review" lessons and missed questions;
  • notification history and data stored on your device.

No data is kept after deletion, except our providers' technical copies (Google Firebase), which are removed according to their own schedules. Deletion is permanent.

See also the privacy policy.
